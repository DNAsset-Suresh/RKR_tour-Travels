Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to drive from Chennai to Tirupati?

The drive from Chennai to Tirupati takes approximately 3.5 to 4 hours (around 135–150 km depending on your Chennai pickup location), plus ~45 minutes uphill to Tirumala.

Does RKR TOUR include TTD darshan tickets?

RKR TOUR provides private chauffeur-driven transport (cars and Tempo Travellers) for your Tirupati and Tirumala journey. Pilgrims arrange TTD darshan tickets separately through the official TTD website.

Is Andhra Pradesh interstate permit included?

Andhra Pradesh entry tax/permit, tolls, and Tirumala parking fees are separate from the base distance tariff and are shared transparently before trip confirmation.
