FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Travel Questions

What vehicles are available for car and van rental in Chennai with RKR TOUR?

RKR TOUR offers 14 chauffeur-driven options in Chennai including Swift Dzire, Toyota Etios, Honda City, Toyota Corolla Altis, Toyota Innova, Innova Crysta, Innova Hycross, Kia Carnival, Toyota Fortuner, Mercedes-Benz E-Class and S-Class, Force Urbania (13-seater), and 12-seater & 18-seater Luxury Tempo Travellers.

How are local rental packages and outstation cab fares calculated?

Local Chennai rentals are available in 5 hours / 50 km and 10 hours / 100 km packages starting from ₹1,300 for Swift Dzire. Outstation fares are calculated transparently by road distance, trip type (one-way, round-trip, or multi-day outstation), and vehicle category using our online Fare Calculator. Tolls, parking, taxes, and driver allowances are confirmed before dispatch.

Which South India destinations can I travel to from Chennai?

Popular outstation routes from Chennai include Ooty, Kodaikanal, Pondicherry, Tirupati, Munnar and Kerala hill stations, Coorg, Madurai, Rameshwaram, and Vellore. Both private cars and group Tempo Travellers are available for custom itineraries.

How do I book a cab or Tempo Traveller with RKR TOUR?

You can calculate an instant estimate using the Fare Calculator and submit a journey request online, call us directly at +91 96294 26655, or send your itinerary via WhatsApp. No upfront online payment is required until vehicle availability and final tariff are confirmed by our team.
