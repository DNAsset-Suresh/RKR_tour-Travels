Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Munnar from Chennai by road?

Munnar is approximately 580–590 km from Chennai via Trichy, Dindigul, and Theni, taking roughly 10 to 11 hours of driving time.

Can we book one vehicle for Munnar, Thekkady, and Alleppey together?

Yes! Multi-day Kerala circuits are one of our most popular outstation bookings. Use the "+ Add more destination" option in our Fare Calculator for multi-stop estimates.

Are Kerala interstate permits and tolls included in the online estimate?

State entry permits, tolls, parking, and driver allowances are excluded from the base distance estimate and confirmed clearly by our team before your trip begins.
