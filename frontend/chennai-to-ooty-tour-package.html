Scenic Nilgiris Road Trip from Chennai (~555 km One Way)

The drive from Chennai to Ooty via Krishnagiri, Salem, Avinashi, and Mettupalayam covers roughly 555 km (about 10–11 hours) and climbs the famous 36 hairpin bends of the Nilgiris. Travelling with an experienced RKR TOUR hill-road chauffeur lets your family relax and enjoy every viewpoint safely.

Customize your 3-day or 4-day Ooty itinerary to include Botanical Gardens, Ooty Lake, Doddabetta Peak, Pykara Falls, Tea Factory, and nearby Coonoor (Sim’s Park & Dolphin’s Nose), or combine Ooty with Mysore or Mudumalai.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Ooty from Chennai by road?

Ooty is approximately 550–560 km from Chennai via Salem and Mettupalayam, taking around 10 to 11 hours of driving time excluding meal stops.

Which vehicle is best for a Chennai to Ooty family trip?

For 2–4 travellers, Swift Dzire, Toyota Etios, or Honda City works well. For 5–7 travellers with luggage, Toyota Innova Crysta or Innova Hycross offers superior ghat comfort. For 8–18 travellers, choose a 12-seater/18-seater Tempo Traveller or Force Urbania.

Does RKR TOUR cover Ooty and Coonoor sightseeing in the same cab booking?

Yes, multi-day round-trip cab bookings include both highway travel and your local Ooty, Coonoor, and Pykara sightseeing stops based on total kilometres and travel days.
