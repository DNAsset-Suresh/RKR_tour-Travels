Enjoy the Coastal Drive via Mahabalipuram (~155 km via ECR)

Pondicherry is Chennai’s favourite coastal getaway — just 150–160 km (3 to 3.5 hours) along the picturesque East Coast Road. Stop en route at UNESCO World Heritage monuments in Mahabalipuram (Shore Temple & Five Rathas) before arriving in French-quarter White Town.

Explore Promenade Beach, Auroville Matrimandir, Sri Aurobindo Ashram, Paradise Beach, and Serenity Beach without worrying about local auto fares or parking.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the distance and travel time from Chennai to Pondicherry?

Via the scenic East Coast Road (ECR), Pondicherry is approximately 155 km from Chennai and takes 3 to 3.5 hours depending on departure point and traffic.

Can we book a one-day Chennai – Mahabalipuram – Pondicherry trip?

Yes! Starting around 6:30–7:00 AM from Chennai allows comfortable sightseeing in Mahabalipuram, Auroville, and Pondicherry White Town with a relaxed evening return.

Do you offer Chennai Airport to Pondicherry direct cab pickups?

Yes, we provide direct pickup from Chennai International/Domestic Airport straight to your hotel or resort in Pondicherry or Auroville.
