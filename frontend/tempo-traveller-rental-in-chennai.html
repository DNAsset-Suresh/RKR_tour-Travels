How much does a 12-seater Tempo Traveller cost in Chennai?

A 12-seater Luxury Tempo Traveller starts at ₹2,500 for a 5 hours / 50 km local package and ₹5,000 for 10 hours / 100 km, with extra distance billed at ₹22/km. Outstation quotes depend on total route distance and travel days.
