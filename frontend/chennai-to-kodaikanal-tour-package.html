Frequently Asked Questions

How many days are recommended for a Chennai to Kodaikanal road trip?

A 3-day / 2-night or 4-day / 3-night itinerary is ideal from Chennai, giving you relaxed travel time plus two full days to explore Kodaikanal viewpoints and lakes.

What is the road distance from Chennai to Kodaikanal?

The road distance is approximately 525 km one way via NH38 (Trichy – Dindigul – Batlagundu – Kodaikanal), taking around 9 to 10 hours.

Can we add Madurai Meenakshi Amman Temple on the return journey?

Yes, Madurai is an easy detour on the return route to Chennai. You can add multiple destinations directly in our Fare Calculator to get a combined route estimate.
